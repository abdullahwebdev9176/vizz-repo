OUR CAPABILITIES

Our Custom Retail Software Development & Omnichannel Commerce Solutions

At Vizz Web Solutions, our software development services for retail cover the complete journey from consulting and planning to development, integration, deployment, and long-term support.

  • Retail Web & Ecommerce Application Development

    We develop custom ecommerce platforms, B2B/B2C marketplaces, and retail web applications that centralize product catalogs, checkout, and account management through secure, responsive interfaces.

    As an application development company, our builds can support multi-store product catalogs, promotions, order tracking, returns, and customer self-service, giving shoppers a consistent experience whether they buy online, in-app, or in-store.

  • Retail Mobile App Development

    Retail teams and shoppers alike need access to information beyond the front counter. Our mobile-first retail solutions extend essential retail workflows to smartphones and tablets, for both customers and staff.

    Our mobile app development services help retailers build mobile applications for customer loyalty and rewards, mobile checkout, clienteling, curbside pickup, and staff-facing tools for inventory lookups, mobile POS, and task management. We create intuitive retail mobile apps that give shoppers and associates faster access to the information, services, and tools they need - wherever they are.

  • Cloud-Based Retail Solutions

    We build cloud-based retail software solutions for organizations that need better scalability, centralized data access, and easier collaboration across stores, warehouses, and digital channels. A cloud retail platform can connect multiple locations, sales channels, and operational data sources within one environment.

    We can also support cloud migration, hybrid architectures, cloud-native application development, data platforms, and API-based integrations. For retailers still dependent on on-premise legacy POS or ERP systems, we can design hybrid solutions that combine existing infrastructure with modern cloud services rather than forcing an unnecessary full migration.

  • AI-Powered Retail Chatbots & Agents

    AI-powered retail solutions can make product discovery and customer support faster and more personal. We develop retail chatbots, conversational commerce assistants, and agentic AI systems that help shoppers find products and help staff resolve routine tasks automatically.

    A retail AI agent can be designed to assist with product search, order status, returns processing, and loyalty program questions. Using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and multi-agent orchestration, these assistants can connect with approved POS, inventory, and CRM systems to provide contextual, real-time answers and even trigger workflows like initiating a return or restocking an item.

  • Retail Software UI/UX Design

    Retail interfaces can undercut sales fast when navigation, checkout, or search feels clunky. Our UI/UX design services focus on frictionless product discovery and checkout flows that convert browsers into buyers, whether on a storefront, a self-service kiosk, or a staff-facing back-office tool.

    We design user experiences for shoppers, sales associates, warehouse staff, and store managers. From wireframes and customer journeys to high-fidelity storefront and dashboard interfaces, we focus on clarity, speed, and conversion.

  • Retail Business Intelligence & Analytics

    Retailers generate large volumes of sales, inventory, and customer data, but that data often sits across POS systems, ecommerce platforms, and disconnected spreadsheets. Our retail analytics solutions help bring this information together into dashboards and actionable insights.

    Depending on the available data, retail BI solutions can help track sell-through rates, basket size, customer lifetime value, inventory turnover, demand forecasts, and store-by-store performance comparisons.

  • Retail API & Third-Party Integration

    Retail environments rarely depend on a single system. POS, ERP, CRM, marketplaces, payment gateways, shipping carriers, and marketing platforms all need to exchange data in real time.

    Our API integration services connect these platforms through secure APIs, middleware, and EDI where required. This reduces duplicate data entry, keeps inventory accurate across channels, and creates a connected retail technology ecosystem.

    We can support integrations with POS, ERP, CRM, marketplaces (Amazon, eBay, Google Shopping), payment gateways, shipping and fulfillment providers, and marketing automation tools.

  • Software Maintenance & Support

    Retail software needs to stay reliable through peak seasons, promotions, and platform updates. Our software maintenance and support services help keep retail applications secure, stable, and aligned with changing business needs.

    Support can include bug resolution, performance optimization, monitoring, security updates, feature development, and integration maintenance. We can also help retailers modernize legacy systems gradually when a full rebuild would create unnecessary operational risk during peak trading periods

AI-Powered Retail Software - Driving the Next Era of Intelligent Commerce

We combine agentic AI, RFID and IoT, computer vision, and cloud computing with practical retail use cases and measurable business outcomes - the technologies shaping the next generation of retail technology solutions.

01

AI-Powered Personalization & Recommendations

We integrate AI in retail software to power product recommendations, dynamic pricing, and personalized offers based on real-time customer behavior. Agentic workflows can also automate inventory checks, supplier coordination, and support escalation, making retail software proactive rather than reactive.

02

RFID Technology & Smart Inventory

RFID technology and connected sensors give retailers granular, real-time visibility into stock levels, shelf availability, and product movement from warehouse to shelf. This data can power automated replenishment, loss-prevention alerts, and self-checkout accuracy without manual stock counts.

03

Computer Vision & Cashierless Retail

Computer vision enables self-checkout, shelf-monitoring, and store-layout optimization by analyzing customer movement and product placement in real time. Retailers can use it to reduce shrinkage, improve planogram compliance, and explore cashierless store formats.

04

Machine Learning & Demand Forecasting

Our machine learning solutions for retail analyze historical and real-time sales data to forecast demand, optimize pricing, and detect fraud. Effective ML solutions are built around reliable data and clearly defined business objectives, from avoiding stockouts to reducing overstock carrying costs.

05

Cloud Computing & Composable Commerce

We use cloud-native, composable architecture to build scalable retail applications, headless storefronts, and centralized data environments that support rapid iteration across web, mobile, and in-store channels.
