COST ESTIMATION & SCOPING

How Much Does Custom Financial Software Development Cost?

The cost of custom financial software development varies significantly based on project scope, complexity, and compliance requirements. Key cost factors include:

  • Factor 01

    Feature Scope & Complexity

    A focused application, like a client portal, costs significantly less than a multi-module core banking or trading platform.

  • Factor 02

    Compliance & Security Requirements

    Solutions requiring extensive regulatory alignment - AML/KYC workflows, SOC 2 audits, PCI DSS certification - require additional design, testing, and documentation effort.

  • Factor 03

    Integrations

    The number and complexity of integrations with core banking systems, payment gateways, market data feeds, or third-party APIs affects both timeline and cost.

  • Factor 04

    Data Migration

    Migrating historical customer, transaction, or portfolio data from legacy systems adds cleansing, transformation, and validation work.

  • Factor 05

    Team Composition

    Projects typically require a project manager, business analyst, UI/UX designers, backend and frontend developers, QA engineers, DevOps engineers, and security specialists.

  • Factor 06

    Deployment Model

    Cloud, on-premises, or hybrid deployment each carry different infrastructure and compliance implications.

  • Factor 07

    Ongoing Support & Maintenance

    Post-launch monitoring, security updates, and regulatory maintenance are an ongoing cost, not a one-time expense.
