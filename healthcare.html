Healthcare Mobile App Development

Healthcare Chatbots & AI Agent Development

AI-powered assistants can make complex healthcare information easier to access for patients and staff alike. We develop healthcare chatbots, LLM-powered virtual health assistants, and agentic AI systems that can help patients, clinicians, and support teams find relevant information faster and act on it automatically.

A healthcare chatbot or AI agent can be designed to assist with symptom triage, appointment scheduling, insurance and billing questions, medication reminders, or clinical documentation support. Using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and multi-agent orchestration built on de-identified or synthetic data, these assistants can connect with approved EHR, scheduling, and billing systems to provide contextual, real-time answers while maintaining PHI security.

Healthcare Software UI/UX Design

Our UI/UX design services for healthcare software focus on simplifying complex clinical workflows and creating interfaces that reduce clinician burden rather than adding to it.

We design ADA- and WCAG-conscious user experiences for physicians, nurses, administrative staff, billing teams, and patients. From wireframes and clinical user journeys to dashboards and high-fidelity interfaces, we focus on clarity, usability, speed, and safe task completion in high-stakes environments.

Healthcare Business Intelligence & Analytics

Healthcare organizations generate large amounts of clinical and operational data, but that data often sits across EHRs, billing systems, labs, and separate applications. Our healthcare analytics solutions help bring this information together and turn it into dashboards, reports, and useful clinical and operational insights.

Depending on the available data, healthcare BI solutions can help track patient outcomes, readmission rates, appointment no-shows, claims denial rates, provider productivity, population health trends, and revenue cycle performance.

HL7, FHIR & Healthcare API Integration

Healthcare environments rarely depend on a single system. EHRs, labs, pharmacies, billing clearinghouses, payers, medical devices, and third-party applications often need to exchange information securely and in real time.

Our healthcare interoperability and API integration services help connect these platforms through HL7 integration, FHIR integration, EDI, and secure middleware. This can reduce duplicate data entry, improve care coordination, and support true healthcare interoperability across the continuum of care.

We can support EHR integration, EMR integration, lab and pharmacy connections, payer and clearinghouse data exchange, medical device and wearable integrations, and custom APIs that bridge proprietary or legacy data models.
